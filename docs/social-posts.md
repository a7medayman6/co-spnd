## LinkedIn

I built **Co-Spnd** because I couldn't find a single expense-tracking app that did two things well: **multiple shared workspaces** and **real analytics** — for free, without bloat.

Every existing tool either forces you into a spreadsheet, locks the good stuff behind a paywall, or buries the numbers so deep you stop caring.

So I built my own.

🔗 **Live:** https://co-spnd-web.vercel.app/
💻 **GitHub:** https://github.com/a7medayman6/co-spnd

---

**What Co-Spnd does:**

📱 **Mobile-first + installable** — fully responsive, designed for one-hand use, and installable as a PWA on your phone. No app store needed.

🏠 **Workspaces** — create multiple shared spending spaces for any context: a trip, a household, a team. Invite members by email, define custom expense split percentages, track who spent what.

💸 **Fast transaction entry** — log an expense in two taps: amount + category. Everything else (description, date, spender) is optional and out of the way.

📊 **Extended analytics** — this is the part I really wanted:
- Total spending by category and by member
- Paste bank messages to auto-fill transactions
- Spending trends over time (daily or monthly granularity)
- Month-over-month comparison with delta %
- Top expenses ranked
- Category trends across multiple months
- Personal analytics across all your workspaces

🗂️ **Custom categories** — not stuck with a fixed list. Add your own.

📥 **CSV import** — bulk-import existing transactions.


---

The stack is intentionally boring: React + Vite + TailwindCSS on the frontend, NestJS + MongoDB on the backend. Mobile-first, fast, and clean.

No subscriptions. No lock-in. Just a useful tool.

If you've ever tried to split a trip with friends and ended up in a spreadsheet rabbit hole — this is for you.

Would love feedback from anyone who gives it a try.

I got frustrated with every expense-splitting app I tried. They either lacked workspace support (so you can track multiple shared contexts like a trip + a household), or the analytics were basically just a pie chart and a total. Nothing gave me both.

So I built **Co-Spnd**.

**Features:**

- **Workspaces** — multiple shared spaces with members, custom split percentages, and isolated transaction history
- **Bank message parsing** — paste in a bank message and it auto-fills the transaction details (amount, date, description)
- **Fast transaction entry** — amount + category, that's it. Optional fields collapse out of the way
- **Analytics that actually tell you something:**
  - Spending by category + by member
  - Daily/monthly spending trends
  - Month-over-month comparison (with delta %)
  - Top expenses
  - Category trends across months
  - Personal analytics across all workspaces
- **Custom categories** — add your own
- **CSV import** — for bulk adding past transactions
- **Mobile-first + installable as a PWA** — works great on your phone, no app store required

It's free, open source, and installable on your phone like a native app. No accounts required to browse the live demo — just sign up with an email.

Happy to answer questions or take feedback. Still early but fully functional.
